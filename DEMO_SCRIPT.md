# 🎬 Aetheria Live Presentation — 3-Minute Demo Click-Path Script

This rock-solid demo script walks an audience through Aetheria from landing to client-side cryptographic unsealing in exactly 3 minutes.

---

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | The Entrance & Visual Philosophy
1. **Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)** in Chrome (maximize window).
2. **Hero Act 0:**
   - Point out the monolithic Swiss typography: *"PRESERVE the unseen. FOREVER"*.
   - Mention the 3D Horological Tourbillon with the glowing orange core.
   - Click the **`[||• SOUND OFF]`** toggle in the pill navbar to turn on the mechanical acoustic ticking.
   - Gently scroll down to reveal the expanding portal transition into **Act 1 (Chamber Manifesto)** and the kinetic ribbons of **Act 2**.

---

### Minute 0:45 – 1:30 | The Timeline & Demo Seeding
1. **Act 3 & 4 (Steps & Archive):**
   - Scroll through the 3D stacked step deck (*Write*, *Seal*, *Awaken*).
   - Enter **Act 4 (Spatial Archive)**: show the live odometer counting through future epochs with the vertical laser spine.
2. **Trigger Demo Mode (Instant Setup):**
   - Press **`D`** on your keyboard (or click **`[D] LOAD DEMO DATA`** in the footer).
   - A toast notification confirms: *"Demo data seeded: 3 capsules loaded into vault"*.
   - The screen smoothly glides down into the **Vault Console (#seal-lock)**.

---

### Minute 1:30 – 2:15 | The Live 20-Second Unseal (The "Magic Moment")
1. In the Vault Console, click the **`VAULT REGISTRY`** tab.
2. Point out the 3 seeded capsules:
   - *A Letter Across Solstices* (`READY TO UNSEAL`)
   - *Temporal Pulse (20s Live Unseal)* (`TIME-LOCKED`)
   - *The Custodian's Classified Archive* (`PASSCODE`)
3. Click **`Inspect`** on **Temporal Pulse (20s Live Unseal)**:
   - Highlight the modal: the ciphertext preview is strictly encrypted, showing zero plaintext.
   - The 4-column horological countdown is ticking down second-by-second in front of the audience: `00d 00h 00m 08s... 07s...`
4. **The Live Dissolution:**
   - As the countdown hits `00:00:00`, watch the lock automatically dissolve without touching the mouse or refreshing the page!
   - Confetti bursts, the status flips to **`UNSEALED`**, and the decrypted message is revealed in full fidelity:
     > *"The lock has dissolved! This memory was encrypted with AES-256-GCM and preserved untouched until this exact second. Welcome to the future."*

---

### Minute 2:15 – 2:40 | Cryptographic Passcode Clearance (PBKDF2)
1. Close the modal (`ESC` or top-right `X`).
2. Click **`Inspect`** on **The Custodian's Classified Archive**:
   - The modal announces: **`PASSCODE REQUIRED TO UNSEAL`**.
   - Type an incorrect passcode: `9999` and click **`UNLOCK`**.
   - Show the inline rejection: *"Invalid passcode. The cryptographic seal rejected your key."*
   - Type the correct key: `1234` and click **`UNLOCK`**.
   - Watch the PBKDF2 key derive on the fly and decrypt the secret coordinates with celebratory confetti.

---

### Minute 2:40 – 3:00 | Forging a New Live Capsule & Wrap-up
1. Close the modal, switch to **`FORGE LOCK`** tab in the console.
2. Click the quick preset **`⚡ 20s (DEMO)`** or **`2045`**.
3. Click a prompt: `+ To my future self`.
4. Press and hold the **`HOLD 1.2s TO ENGRAVE & SEAL`** button:
   - The orange fill bar expands as you hold down.
   - Release: the VDF hexadecimal cipher stream engages.
   - The **Immutable Sealed Receipt** renders with the unique SHA-512 verification key.
5. Click **`Export Certificate`** to show the generated Swiss provenance text file.
6. Scroll down to the monumental footer and highlight the clean CTA:
   - **`SEAL A MEMORY ↗`**
   - End on the honesty note: *"Time-lock enforced client-side by Aetheria. Content encrypted in-browser using AES-GCM 256 before storage."*
